# Per-layer pass@10 chart with the fitted phrase rows (Qwen3.5-9B)

Date: 2026-09-20. Code `modal_exp/jlens_chart.py` (Modal app `jlens-chart`, volume `jlens-multitoken`, dir `/vol/chart`).
Prompts `modal_exp/chart_prompts/*.json`. Results `modal_exp/results/pass10.json`, `fit_report.json`, chart `results/pass10_chart.png`.

## What the source chart is

Ryan's `chart.webp` is Figure "DeepSeek-V4-Flash" from Camila Blank's Alignment Forum post *R-lens: Making J-lens More
Faithful on Early Layers*: per-layer pass@10 (is the intermediate in the lens top-10 at the bolded position) for R-lens,
logit lens and J-lens on five prompt families (multihop, multilingual, association, typo, poetry; n=50 each). The post
gives one example prompt per family and no prompt files. DeepSeek-V4-Flash is a 284B / 13B-active FP8 MoE (160 GB), so
adding a series to *that* chart means a multi-GPU run; this experiment adds our series on Qwen3.5-9B instead, with her
own J-lens / R-lens artifacts for that model (`camilablank/workspace-lenses`, `qwen3.5-9b/{j,r}-lens/lens.pt`,
post-trained `Qwen/Qwen3.5-9B`, target layer 30, n=25 pile-10k prompts).

## Setup

* Families: her five, authored to her examples (50 each; readout at the bolded token = the last prompt token, or the
  token before the final "is"/"es"/"est"/"ist" for multihop/multilingual), plus **multihop_mt**: 50 two-hop questions
  whose intermediate is a multi-token entity (New Zealand, Costa Rica, Abraham Lincoln, ...). multihop = the 50 two-hop
  questions from `RLENS_2026-09-12.md`. multihop / multihop_mt / multilingual keep only items the model answers
  correctly (greedy, 8 tokens): 43 / 44 / 46 of 50 kept.
* Under the Qwen3.5 tokenizer, items with at least one multi-token alias: multihop 2, multihop_mt 37 (31 after
  filtering), association 14, multilingual 0, typo 0 (even "accommodation" is one token), poetry 2. 57 distinct
  multi-token phrases.
* Rows: bias-free logistic regression over the extended softmax (the PR recipe), 300 FineWeb contexts per phrase
  (40 held out), 8 in-context negatives per context, 900 generic FineWeb docs x 512 tokens (100 held out), L2 1e-6,
  L-BFGS. Held-out report: mean AUROC 0.976 (weakest 0.94: George Washington, Amazon River), generic top-10 rate
  1e-4, mass on new tokens 2.2x the corpus prior.
* Series: R-lens, logit lens, J-lens with the post's hit rule (first token of any alias in the real-vocab top-10);
  **J-lens + phrase rows** and **R-lens + phrase rows**: top-10 over the vocabulary extended with the 57 rows, hit if
  the item's phrase row or a single-token alias is in it; **control**: a *wrong* phrase row in the J-lens top-10.
  Where an item has no multi-token alias the "+ rows" series reduces to the standard one.

## Results, base variant (rows fitted on final-layer states; pass10.json, pass10_chart.png)

| family | n | multi-token items | peak J-lens | peak J+rows | rows-only peak | first layer with pass@10 >= 0.3: J / J+rows |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| multihop | 43 | 2 | 0.67 @L27 | 0.67 @L27 | 0.02 | 8 / 8 |
| multihop_mt | 44 | 31 | 0.75 @L28 | 0.75 @L24 | 0.39 @L27 | 6 / 6 (0.47 vs 0.63 at L9) |
| multilingual | 46 | 0 | 0.15 | 0.15 | 0 | never |
| association | 50 | 14 | 0.34 @L24 | 0.34 @L24 | 0.16 @L9 | 24 / 9 |
| typo | 50 | 0 | 0.74 @L22 | 0.74 @L22 | 0 | 7 / 7 |
| poetry | 50 | 2 | 0.76 @L31 | 0.76 @L31 | 0 | 22 / 22 |

* The post's qualitative picture reproduces on Qwen3.5-9B: R-lens above J-lens in the early layers on multihop and
  typo, logit lens far below both until the last layers, multilingual near zero, poetry only in the last third.
* The phrase rows change the picture only where intermediates are multi-token, as they must: multihop_mt mid-layers
  (+0.16 at L9, +0.07 at L24) and association (the concept enters the top-10 at layer 9 rather than 24).
* Hit rule for "+ rows" (final): top-10 over the extended vocabulary, hit if the post's rule fires there or the item's
  phrase row is in it, so the series is a superset of the J-lens up to the few real tokens the rows displace. (A first
  pass counted only the row or a single-token alias and therefore sat *below* the J-lens at the last layers on
  multihop_mt, 0.34 vs 0.55 at L30: the model's own logits carry " New", not the "New Zealand" row.)
* Corrected numbers, J-lens vs J-lens + rows: multihop_mt 0.43 -> 0.66 at L8-10, 0.66 -> 0.80 at L24, 0.75 -> 0.75 at
  L28; association 0.04 -> 0.16 at L8, 0.22 -> 0.28 at L10, 0.34 -> 0.34 at L24. Other families unchanged (no
  multi-token intermediates).
* **Control (a wrong phrase row in the J-lens top-10)**: 0.65-1.00 at layers 0-8 in every family, 0.45 at L8-10 and
  0.14 at L24 on multihop_mt, 0.66-0.82 across L8-24 on association. So below layer ~12 the extended top-10 is mostly
  phrase rows and the green gains there are not interpretable; the L24 gain on multihop_mt (rows-only 0.30 vs control
  0.14) is the credible part. Cause: the rows are fitted on final-layer states only, and lens-transported early-layer
  states are off that distribution (the benchmark saw the same at 6-14% with 15 rows; with 57 rows it is near-certain).
## Variant `ln`: lens-transported generic states (layers 2-24, J- and R-lens, 100 docs, 555k positions) as extra negatives

Final-layer quality unchanged (held-out AUROC 0.977, generic top-10 2e-4, mass/prior 2.5). The control drops to 0.00
at every layer in every family (0.05 at L0), so the crowding is gone; but the rows also stop firing at real pre-intermediate
positions under the lens: rows-only pass@10 on multihop_mt 0.02 at L8-10 and 0.05-0.11 at L26-28 (base: 0.32 / 0.36 /
0.32), association 0.00 everywhere. "J-lens + rows" == J-lens to two decimals on every panel. Diagnosis: shown lens-transported
states only as negatives, the rows learn "final-layer state or not" rather than "this phrase or not" - a one-sided fix.
Files `pass10_ln.json`, `pass10_chart_ln.png`, `fit_report_ln.json`.

## Variant `lp`: lens-transported states on both sides

Same negatives as `ln`, plus the lens-transported *pre-phrase* states of the training contexts at layers 2-24 as positives
(269k, target = the phrase row). This treats the lens readout at each layer as a prediction of the eventual output, which
is how the J-lens uses W_U itself. Final-layer quality unchanged (AUROC 0.977, generic top-10 1e-4, mass/prior 2.2).
Files `pass10_lp.json`, `pass10_chart_lp.png`, `fit_report_lp.json`.

* The rows now read the multi-token intermediate at the layers where the J-lens reads its first token, and earlier:
  multihop_mt +0.23 to +0.32 over layers 8-20 and +0.07 to +0.14 at 24-28; association +0.08 to +0.20 from layer 8 on.
  R-lens + rows is the top curve on multihop_mt (0.77 at L10-12, 0.84 at L24-26).
* But the wrong-row control is high again (0.5-0.8 at layers 8-16 on the three multihop/association panels), i.e. the
  rows crowd the extended top-10 at lens layers, and on plain multihop they displace the real first token (-0.05 at
  L8-12).

## Matched control and verdict

Matched control = a *wrong* phrase's first token (the real-vocab side of the same 57 concepts: " New", " South",
" United", " Abraham", " climate", ...) in the J-lens real top-10. It is 0.00-0.09 at every layer in every family.
(A first version with the first tokens of all ~300 intermediates read 0.2-0.5 on most families, but that pool includes
common words like " small", " cold", " dog"; the per-token pool is the fair one.)

| family, layers | J-lens | + rows (lp) | rows only | wrong row (lp) | wrong row (base) | matched (real vocab) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| multihop_mt, L8-20 | 0.41-0.45 | 0.66-0.73 | 0.48-0.57 | 0.48-0.77 | 0.25-0.55 | 0.02-0.05 |
| multihop_mt, L24-28 | 0.66-0.75 | 0.80-0.82 | 0.45-0.50 | 0.25-0.30 | 0.07-0.14 | 0.02 |
| association, L10-28 | 0.00-0.34 | 0.14-0.42 | 0.14-0.24 | 0.24-0.80 | 0.18-0.74 | 0.00-0.02 |
| multihop (2 mt items), L6-16 | 0.26-0.40 | 0.26-0.35 | 0.00 | 0.72-0.86 | 0.16-0.86 | 0.00-0.02 |
| typo / poetry / multilingual | unchanged | unchanged | 0 | <=0.30 early, ~0 late | | ~0 |

Verdict for "our approach on this chart":
1. The rows add a readout the standard lenses cannot give: the multi-token intermediate itself, at the layers where
   the J-lens shows only its first token, and earlier (multihop_mt +0.2-0.3 pass@10 over layers 8-20 with `lp`, +0.14
   at L24 with base; association concept rows from layer 8).
2. At intermediate layers the rows are much less selective than real unembedding rows: with 57 rows, a wrong row is in
   the extended top-10 for 50-80% of multihop/association prompts, where a wrong phrase's first token is in the real
   top-10 for ~2%. The rows act partly as category detectors ("a country/state/president is coming") rather than
   phrase detectors; this is the sibling-bleed weakness the benchmark measured at the final layer (AUROC 0.73 vs
   first-token siblings), amplified at lens layers.
3. Rows fitted on final-layer states only (base) are selective from layer ~20 on (wrong-row 0.07-0.14 at L24-28 vs
   matched 0.02) and their late gain (+0.14 at L24 on multihop_mt) is the part that survives the control.
4. Fitting with lens-transported negatives only (`ln`) removes all false positives and all true positives.
So the honest series for the chart is base or lp *with the two control lines drawn*; the chart delivered is the lp
one (`pass10_chart_lp.png`), the base one (`pass10_chart.png`) has the same controls.

Not done: the same on DeepSeek-V4-Flash (needs 2-4 H100/H200 and the DeepseekV4 HF path; her lenses exist on the Hub);
a sibling-aware fit (e.g. contrastive term between rows sharing a first token) to fix (2); the copy-confound variant.
