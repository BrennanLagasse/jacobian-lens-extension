# R-lens on Qwen3.5-9B-Base: pass@10, direction ablation, and the phrase benchmark

Date: 2026-09-12. Code `modal_exp/jlens_rlens.py` (Modal app `jlens-rlens`, volume `jlens-multitoken`,
lenses at `/vol/lenses/base_{jlens,rlens}_t30.pt`). Tables `modal_exp/results/RLENS.md`; raw
`rlens_attribution.json`, `rlens_phrases.json`. Source: camilablank, agam_bhatia, Neel Nanda, "R-lens:
making J-lens more faithful on early layers" (Alignment Forum); artifacts `camilablank/workspace-lenses`.

## What R-lens is, and what was built

R-lens is the jlens estimator with three LRP rules installed in the backward pass (RelP, arXiv
2508.21258): LN-rule (the RMSNorm rsqrt scale is a constant in the backward pass), identity-rule (the
sigmoid factor of SiLU is detached, so the activation's backward is per-element linear), half-rule (the
SwiGLU product's gradient is split evenly across the two branches, β=0.5). The provenance in the
authors' `qwen3.5-9b/r-lens/lens.pt` fixes the config: those three rules, q/k norms untouched, the
GatedDeltaNet gated norms untouched, target layer 30 (penultimate), 25 prompts from `NeelNanda/pile-10k`
at 128 tokens, skip_first 4.

I implemented the rules as instance-level forward overrides on the two residual RMSNorms per block,
the final norm and the gated MLP, grafting the RelP gradient onto the standard forward value so the
forward pass is bit-identical (max |diff| = 0.0; a first attempt that recomputed silu as g·σ(g) in
bf16 drifted by 1% over 32 layers). J-lens and R-lens were then fitted on `Qwen/Qwen3.5-9B-Base` with
the authors' recipe (25 prompts, target 30, 16 output dims per backward pass; 21 min each on an H100).
The R-lens per-prompt Jacobian norms are about half the J-lens's (max ‖J‖/√d 2.9 vs 5.6 at the last
prompt), as expected from the half-rule and the dropped normalisation terms.

## 1. pass@10 on two-hop questions

50 base-model two-hop prompts of the form "Fact: The capital of the country where the Eiffel Tower
stands is" with a latent intermediate (France) and an answer (Paris). pass@10 = the intermediate's
first token is in the lens top-10 at the penultimate position (the word before "is").

| layers | R-lens | J-lens | logit lens |
|---|---|---|---|
| mean over all 32 layers | **0.341** | 0.312 | 0.109 |
| mean over layers 0-15 | **0.264** | 0.236 | 0.024 |
| L4 / L6 | 0.14 / 0.32 | 0.06 / 0.26 | 0.00 / 0.08 |
| L8-L16 | 0.32-0.40 | 0.36 | 0.00-0.08 |
| L22 / L24 / L26 / L28 | 0.28 / 0.64 / 0.78 / 0.72 | 0.18 / 0.56 / 0.68 / 0.72 | 0.04 / 0.44 / 0.46 / 0.44 |

Both lenses read the intermediate from layer 6 on, where the logit lens shows nothing until layer 24.
R-lens leads J-lens at the very early layers (4-6) and at 22-26; J-lens is marginally ahead at 12-16.
The direction matches the post; the size of the gain on this 9B dense base model is modest (+0.03
mean pass@10), and the post itself reports the largest gains on its big MoE.

## 2. Direction ablation (the attribution experiment)

For each question and each lens, the unit direction d_l = J_lᵀ(γ ⊙ W_U[t]) of the intermediate's first
token t (γ = final-norm gain; J = I for the logit lens and for layers ≥ 30) is projected out of the
residual at the penultimate token during the prompt forward pass, at either the first half of the
layers or all of them, and at the penultimate position alone or together with the last position.
8 samples per prompt at T=0.7, top-p 0.95, 12 new tokens; accuracy = an answer alias appears in the
continuation. 43 of 50 questions have baseline accuracy ≥ 0.5 (mean 0.907) and enter the summary.
A random unit direction per layer is the control. 95% intervals are bootstraps over questions.

| direction | layers | positions | acc after | relative loss [95% CI] |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-lens | first half | −2 | 0.811 | **+0.106** [+0.006, +0.218] |
| J-lens | first half | −2 | 0.805 | **+0.112** [+0.019, +0.221] |
| logit lens | first half | −2 | 0.924 | −0.019 [−0.062, +0.019] |
| random | first half | −2 | 0.919 | −0.013 [−0.057, +0.023] |
| R-lens | all | −2 | 0.759 | **+0.163** [+0.060, +0.274] |
| J-lens | all | −2 | 0.779 | **+0.141** [+0.045, +0.252] |
| logit lens | all | −2 | 0.910 | −0.003 [−0.038, +0.028] |
| random | all | −2 | 0.939 | −0.035 [−0.080, +0.006] |
| R-lens | all | −2, −1 | 0.448 | +0.506 [+0.386, +0.626] |
| J-lens | all | −2, −1 | 0.398 | +0.561 [+0.433, +0.685] |
| logit lens | all | −2, −1 | 0.605 | +0.333 [+0.224, +0.446] |
| random | all | −2, −1 | 0.927 | −0.022 [−0.073, +0.019] |

Paired R-minus-J extra loss: first half/−2: −0.006 [−0.038, +0.023]; all/−2: +0.020 [−0.017, +0.058];
all/−2,−1: −0.049 [−0.090, −0.009].

What this says:

* **The lens directions are causally load-bearing; the logit-lens direction is not.** A single rank-1
  removal per layer at one token position costs 14-16% of two-hop accuracy for either lens, and
  10-11% when only the first 16 layers are touched, while the logit-lens and random directions cost
  nothing (their intervals include zero). Several questions go from 8/8 to 0/8 (Egypt, Australia,
  Brazil, Canada, Spain) under either lens direction and stay at 8/8 under the controls. This is the
  qualitative result of the post's attribution figure.
* **R-lens and J-lens are not distinguishable on this model.** The paired differences at the
  penultimate position are within ±0.02 with intervals spanning zero; with the last position included,
  J-lens hurts slightly more (significant at the 5% level). The post's "much larger" R-lens ablation
  effect does not reproduce on Qwen3.5-9B-Base with 43 questions and alias-matched accuracy; their
  result was on larger instruction-tuned models with an autorater. On this model the early-layer
  faithfulness gain shows up in the readout (section 1 and 3), not in the ablation.
* **Position matters.** Removing the direction at the last token too doubles the damage and makes
  even the logit-lens direction bite (33%), because at the answer position the intermediate is already
  represented in the unembedding basis at late layers. The post's choice of the penultimate position
  is what isolates the lens-specific contribution.

## 3. The phrase benchmark under the matched lenses

Held-out profiles of the phrase benchmark (2,942 contexts, 15 phrases) recomputed under the matched
J-lens/R-lens pair and the logit lens. The bias-free logistic phrase head was refit from the stored
states (row norms 2.23, identical to the benchmark run).

| lens | token | L4 | L8 | L12 | L16 | L20 | L24 | L28 | L30 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-lens | real first token | 0.00 | 0.01 | 0.01 | 0.02 | 0.10 | **0.23** | **0.41** | 0.44 |
| J-lens | real first token | 0.00 | 0.02 | 0.03 | 0.03 | 0.10 | 0.20 | 0.39 | 0.44 |
| logit | real first token | 0.00 | 0.00 | 0.00 | 0.01 | 0.04 | 0.19 | 0.36 | 0.44 |
| R-lens | phrase, LR no bias | **0.00** | **0.08** | 0.10 | 0.15 | 0.30 | 0.46 | 0.50 | 0.45 |
| J-lens | phrase, LR no bias | 0.06 | 0.17 | 0.20 | 0.22 | 0.33 | 0.45 | 0.53 | 0.45 |
| R-lens | phrase, Brennan proj | 0.03 | 0.36 | 0.55 | 0.67 | 0.80 | 0.87 | 0.88 | 0.91 |
| J-lens | phrase, Brennan proj | 0.13 | 0.67 | 0.82 | 0.82 | 0.87 | 0.89 | 0.90 | 0.91 |
| R-lens | phrase in top-10, first token not | 0.00 | 0.08 | 0.09 | 0.14 | 0.24 | 0.26 | 0.14 | 0.10 |

* **R-lens removes the early-layer phrase-head hits that the benchmark flagged as suspect.** Under the
  J-lens the bias-free phrase row was in the top-10 at 6% / 17% / 20% of contexts at layers 4 / 8 / 12,
  where the real first token is at 0-3%; under the R-lens it is 0% / 8% / 10%. The late-layer curves
  are unchanged. This is the post's claim, seen on our data: the J-lens transports early residuals into
  directions that spuriously align with a learned row, and the RelP backward does so less.
* **R-lens surfaces the real first token slightly earlier** (0.23 / 0.30 / 0.41 vs 0.20 / 0.27 / 0.39
  at layers 24 / 26 / 28), the same ordering as in the two-hop pass@10.
* **Brennan's head is still saturated under R-lens** (36% at layer 8, 87% at layer 24); the
  calibration problem is in the row, not the lens.
* Absolute numbers differ from the earlier benchmark tables because those used the Hub Base lens
  (target 31, 458 prompts); the matched pair here targets layer 30 with 25 prompts, so layer 30 is
  read without transport.

## Recommendation

* Use the R-lens as the default readout for phrase heads on this model: same fit cost, same forward,
  cleaner early layers; the early-layer "phrase surfaces before its first token" population shrinks to
  8-14% at layers 8-16 and should be examined there rather than at layer 4.
* Keep the ablation protocol (penultimate position, first-half vs all layers, random and logit-lens
  controls, bootstrap over questions) as the causal check for any new head or lens; it separates lens
  directions from controls cleanly at n = 43.
* Do not expect the R-vs-J ablation gap the post reports on a 9B dense base model; test it on
  Qwen3.6-27B or an MoE if that comparison matters.

## Caveats

* 50 hand-written two-hop questions, 8 samples each, alias matching instead of an autorater; 7 questions
  the base model cannot answer were excluded.
* One direction per layer is removed; the post's exact projection operator is not specified, so
  d_l = J_lᵀ(γ ⊙ W_U[t]) with a unit-norm projection is my reading of "the projection of the lens
  direction for the intermediate token".
* Lenses are fitted on 25 prompts as in the authors' artifacts; more prompts would tighten both.
